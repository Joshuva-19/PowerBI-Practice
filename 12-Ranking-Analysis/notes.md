\# Ranking \& Conditional Analysis



\## Topics Practiced



\- RANKX()

\- Ranking products by sales

\- Ranking products by profit

\- ALL()

\- Conditional DAX

\- IF()

\- Sorting and analyzing ranked results



\## Measures Created



\- Total Sales

\- Total Profit

\- Sales Rank

\- Profit Rank

\- Sales Performance



\## Analysis



Created a ranking table to compare products based on total sales and total profit.



The ranking measures were used to identify higher- and lower-performing products.



A conditional measure was also created to classify sales performance into High and Low categories.



\## What I Learned



RANKX() can be used in DAX to rank values based on a selected measure.



The ALL() function can be used to remove filters from a column or table when calculating rankings.



Conditional DAX expressions such as IF() can be used to classify business performance based on defined conditions.



\## Business Use



Ranking analysis can help businesses identify top-performing products and compare sales and profit performance.
