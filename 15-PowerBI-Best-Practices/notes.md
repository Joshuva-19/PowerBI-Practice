\## What I Learned



A well-designed Power BI report should be simple, organized, readable,

and focused on useful business information.



Good data modeling, appropriate calculations, meaningful visuals, and

effective report navigation help improve the quality and usability of

a Power BI report.



Performance and usability should be considered throughout the report

development process rather than only after the dashboard is completed.
