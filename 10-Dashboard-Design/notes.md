\# Dashboard Design \& Business Insights



\## Dashboard



Created a Sales Analytics Dashboard in Power BI by combining the concepts practiced in previous sessions.



\## KPIs



\- Total Sales

\- Total Profit

\- Total Orders

\- Profit Margin



\## Visualizations



\- Sales by Category

\- Sales by Region

\- Monthly Sales Trend

\- Product Sales and Profit



\## Filters



\- Region

\- Category



\## Dashboard Design



\- Arranged visuals in a clean and organized layout

\- Added meaningful titles to visuals

\- Used KPI cards to highlight important business metrics

\- Formatted numbers for better readability

\- Aligned visuals to improve the overall dashboard appearance

\- Used slicers for interactive analysis

\- Checked interactions between slicers and visuals

\- Avoided unnecessary visuals and kept the dashboard simple



\## Business Analysis



The dashboard can be used to analyze sales performance across different categories, regions, products, and time periods.



Key areas analyzed:



\- Overall sales performance

\- Overall profit performance

\- Number of orders

\- Profit margin

\- Category-wise sales

\- Region-wise sales

\- Monthly sales trends

\- Product-level sales and profit



\## What I Learned



\- A dashboard should present important information clearly and concisely.

\- KPI cards help users quickly understand overall performance.

\- Charts help identify patterns and differences in the data.

\- Slicers allow users to interactively explore the dashboard.

\- Good dashboard design improves readability and makes business analysis easier.

\- Business insights should be based on actual data rather than assumptions.

Built a basic interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard by combining data cleaning, data modeling, DAX measures, visualizations, filters, slicers, and dashboard design concepts.
